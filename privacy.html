Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who we are

SellPilot AI is an advertising software service being developed by TRI TECH SERVICES LLC in the United States. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@tritechla.com.

Information we process

Depending on the features used, SellPilot AI may collect or process account information such as email addresses, securely hashed passwords, subscription identifiers, product details, advertising campaign drafts, and information supplied by users. We may also process technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide requested services, manage accounts and subscriptions, create advertising materials, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and communicate about the service. We do not claim that all advertised integrations are currently operational.

Advertising platform connections

SellPilot AI is developing integrations with advertising platforms, including Meta services such as Facebook and Instagram advertising. When a connection feature becomes available, users will be asked to authorize the applicable access. Authorized advertising account, campaign, and performance information will be processed to deliver the features the user requests. Access will be subject to the relevant platform permissions and policies.

Service providers

SellPilot AI uses third-party infrastructure and services to support application hosting, data processing, and payments. These may include Render, Stripe, and authorized advertising or AI service providers, depending on which features are enabled. Their handling of information is governed by applicable agreements and policies.

Data storage and security

Our application is designed to store account and service information in a database and to use password hashes rather than storing account passwords in plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Additional controls and procedures are being evaluated before wider launch.

Retention and deletion

We retain information as needed to operate the service, meet applicable obligations, and address legitimate security or operational needs. To request account closure, access to personal information, correction, or deletion, email info@tritechla.com. We will review and respond to requests, verify identity where appropriate, and explain any information that must be retained. Automated in-app account deletion is not currently confirmed as available.

Third-party services and choices

Users may stop using integrations or revoke permissions through the applicable third-party platform where supported. Revoking access does not necessarily delete information previously processed by SellPilot AI; users may contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SellPilot AI develops. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above and, where appropriate, by providing additional notice.
